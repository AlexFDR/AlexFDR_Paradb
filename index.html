How does it work?

Because song complexity varies wildly from track to track, don't assume that just because a song doesn't have crazy fills or reality bending time signatures doesn't mean that a song's “Easy to chart”. The difficulty to chart a song comes primarily from if it's quantized or not. Non quantized tracks can double the amount of time it takes to chart it.

Good rule of thumb, if a song's by Lars Ulrich, or from before the mid 80's, or is from a relatively unknown or small band (at the time it was recorded) it's most likely not quantized, meaning it's going to be a nightmare to chart so it'll cost more.

Ignoring a few outliers, all of my charts use my own custom kit so that anyone that wants to play my charts only needs to make their kit once and have all of them work out of the box without having to move pieces and stuff. I might do custom kits every once in a while but that's on a case by case basis, just keep in mind that since i'm pretty much out of a VR headset right now it'll come at a significant premium since i'd need to ask either mother or fencil to make one and i think they should get something for their time. Pricing

My rate is $20 base + $2 'service fee' + $1 per 10 minutes spent charting the track, which doubles for multiple difficulties with a minimum of $2. For example, if i spent 2 hours charting an Expert difficulty, 30 minutes charting a Hard difficulty, and 2 minutes charting medium, the total would be $20+$2+$12+$6+$2, $42.

I both increased the pricing for extra difficulties and added a minimum because i realized that, in most cases, i wouldn't even get paid for multiple difficulties on some songs since they are sometimes really quick turn arounds but it still takes me work on them.

For a full albums/6+ songs, the base price goes down to $10 per song while everything else stays the same. In these cases i'll take the base price upfront, with the rest being paid every 3 songs delivered.

Because of how i price things, i can't give estimates on how expensive a track will be until I'm done.

If you want to have a basic idea on how much a few of my charts would have cost, take a look at # / alexfdr-portfolio for some examples (edited)
